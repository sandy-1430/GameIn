
# GameIn Sponsorship Flow

This document outlines the step-by-step flow for how a brand sponsors a creator on the GameIn platform.

---

## 🎯 Flow Steps

1. **Brand Searches Creators**
   - Brands use the search functionality to discover creators based on filters like genre, rating, viewer count, etc.

2. **View Creator Profile & Offerings**
   - Brand clicks on a creator's profile to view available sponsorship offerings such as:
     - Logo Streaming
     - Social Media Shoutouts
     - Branded Merchandise
     - Video Commercials

3. **Select Sponsorship Offering**
   - Brand selects one of the offerings created by the creator.

4. **Choose Sponsorship Type**
   - Option to select:
     - **Regular Sponsorship**
     - **Prize Pool Sponsorship**

5. **Set Start & End Dates**
   - Brand provides a schedule for the campaign duration.

6. **Pay via Payment Gateway**
   - Integrated with Stripe or Razorpay.
   - Calculates: `total = basePrice + 5% platform fee + 15.3% tax`

7. **Sponsorship Confirmed**
   - Upon successful payment, the sponsorship is confirmed.

8. **Brand ↔ Creator Chat Opens**
   - A private chat is enabled for discussing creative brief and campaign execution.

9. **Brand Tracks Insights**
   - Platform dashboard shows:
     - View Count
     - Click Engagement
     - Conversion/Sales Data (via UTM/Affiliate Links)

---

## ✅ Summary

This sponsorship flow ensures:
- Transparent pricing
- Easy collaboration
- Real-time insights tracking
- Professional and creator-friendly experience
